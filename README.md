# ArtCraft

**Creative studio for images, video and 3D**

🌐 Website: [ArtCraft](https://artcraft.tools/)

## About

ArtCraft is a web creative studio that puts visual control before generation. Instead of relying only on a short prompt, you can arrange layers, stage objects and a camera in a 3D scene, work a video timeline, build moodboards and keep editable projects in a private library.

Local editing and project saving do not submit an AI job and do not spend generation credits. When you want AI output—images, video, audio, mesh or world generation, plus AI editing such as masked redraws, video background edits or character training—you pick a supported model, review the credit estimate and submit. Images and videos use the models listed in the studio; three-dimensional worlds use World Labs Marble; available audio and object generation use Fal.

Pricing is credit-based with monthly subscriptions (Lite $29.90 / 600 credits, Standard $49.90 / 1,500, Pro $99.90 / 3,600, Max $199.90 / 8,000), yearly plans at about 50% less, and one-time credit packs. Trending templates cost credits per output. Failed jobs return reserved credits. The product is aimed at artists who want to build the scene first, then generate, all in the browser.

## Features

- Manual image editing, drawing and 3D scene composition
- Video timelines, moodboards and frame extraction
- Private saved projects and artwork
- Credit-based AI image, video, audio, mesh and world generation
- Credit-based AI editing and character training
- Credit quotes shown before you submit
